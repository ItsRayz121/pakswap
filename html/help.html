How Escrow Works on PakSwap

Last updated: 01 May 2026 · 5 min read

PakSwap uses a secure escrow system to protect every P2P trade. Here's how it works:

Step 1: USDT Locked in Escrow

When a buyer initiates a trade, the seller's cryptocurrency is automatically locked in PakSwap's escrow. This means the seller's USDT cannot be moved, withdrawn, or sent elsewhere until the trade completes.

🔒
Funds are 100% safe in escrow
Neither party can access the crypto during the trade

Step 2: Buyer Sends Payment

The buyer sends PKR to the seller via the agreed payment method (JazzCash, bank transfer, etc.). The buyer then clicks "I've Paid" and can optionally upload a payment screenshot.

Step 3: Seller Verifies & Releases

The seller checks their JazzCash or bank account. Once payment is confirmed, the seller clicks "Release USDT." The crypto is instantly sent from escrow to the buyer's PakSwap wallet.

What if there's a problem?

If the seller doesn't release after payment, the buyer can open a dispute. Our team reviews evidence from both parties and resolves within 4 hours. The USDT stays locked in escrow throughout — it's always safe.

⚠️ Never cancel a trade after sending payment. If you sent payment but want to cancel, open a dispute instead. Cancelling will return the USDT to the seller.

Popular Questions

How does escrow protect my trade?▲
When you initiate a buy, the seller's USDT is locked by PakSwap. It cannot be released until you confirm payment, or a dispute agent decides. You are always protected.
My KYC was rejected — what do I do?▼
Check the rejection reason in your KYC status page. Common issues: blurry CNIC photo, expired CNIC, name mismatch. Retake photos in good lighting and resubmit.
Seller is not releasing USDT after I paid▼
Open a dispute immediately via the trade room. Upload your payment proof (JazzCash screenshot with transaction ID). Our team will review and release your USDT within 4 hours.
Can I use someone else's JazzCash account?▼
No. Payment account names must match your KYC name. Third-party payments are prohibited and will result in dispute loss and account restriction.
