How to Complete a P2P Trade

Overview

P2P (peer-to-peer) trading on PakSwap lets you buy or sell crypto directly with another user. PakSwap holds the seller's crypto in escrow and releases it only after a human admin confirms the buyer's payment. This protects both sides from fraud.

✅ Quick summary: Browse ads → Start trade → Send payment via JazzCash/Easypaisa/Bank → Upload screenshot → Wait for admin to release crypto → Done.

Before You Start

Make sure you have completed the following before your first trade:

  • Phone verification (Basic KYC) — required for any trading
  • CNIC verification (Standard KYC) — required for trades above 50,000 PKR/day
  • A linked payment method: JazzCash, Easypaisa, or bank account
  • Sufficient balance in your payment app (for buying) or crypto wallet (for selling)
💡 Your CNIC name must match the name registered on your JazzCash/Easypaisa/bank account. Mismatched names are a common reason for payment disputes.

Step-by-Step: Buying Crypto

1
Browse the Marketplace

Go to Marketplace and use the filters to find SELL ads matching your preferred coin, payment method, and price. Look for merchants with a gold badge and high completion rates for the safest experience.

2
Enter Amount and Start Trade

Click on a SELL ad, enter the PKR amount you want to spend (must be within the ad's min/max limits), and click Buy Now. The seller's crypto is immediately locked in escrow.

3
Send Payment

Send the exact PKR amount to the seller's JazzCash, Easypaisa, or bank account (shown in the trade window). Use your registered account — do not send from a third party's account.

⚠️ Send the exact amount. Sending a different amount (even 1 PKR less) may cause the admin to reject the payment confirmation.
4
Upload Payment Screenshot

In the trade window, click I've Paid and upload a clear screenshot of your payment confirmation. The screenshot must show: your name, recipient name, amount, transaction ID, and date/time.

5
Wait for Admin Verification

A PakSwap admin will review your screenshot against the payment record. This usually takes 5–15 minutes during business hours (9am–9pm PKT). Do not close the trade window.

6
Crypto Released to Your Wallet

Once admin confirms the payment, the crypto is released from escrow to your PakSwap wallet instantly. You'll receive an SMS and in-app notification. The trade is now complete.

Step-by-Step: Selling Crypto

When you sell, you are the one who creates an ad (or responds to a BUY ad). The flow is reversed:

  1. Post a SELL ad on the marketplace (or respond to a BUY ad)
  2. A buyer initiates a trade — your crypto is locked in escrow immediately
  3. The buyer sends payment to your JazzCash/bank account
  4. The buyer uploads their payment screenshot
  5. Admin verifies the screenshot — once confirmed, you receive the PKR and the buyer gets the crypto
  6. Do not cancel or request to cancel once you've received payment
⚠️ Never release early. Do not manually confirm a trade before admin verifies the payment, even if the buyer sends you a screenshot directly in chat. Only admin-confirmed payments trigger a legitimate release.

The Payment Window Timer

Every trade has a payment window timer — typically 15, 30, or 45 minutes depending on what the ad seller has set. This is visible in the trade window as a countdown.

  • If the buyer does not mark "I've Paid" before the timer expires, the trade is automatically cancelled and crypto is returned to the seller
  • If you (the buyer) need more time due to a bank delay, use the in-trade chat to request an extension before the timer hits zero
  • Extensions are at the seller's discretion

How Escrow Works

Escrow is PakSwap's core safety mechanism. Here's exactly what happens under the hood:

  • When a trade starts, the seller's crypto moves from their PakSwap wallet to a locked escrow ledger entry
  • Neither the buyer, the seller, nor the escrow holder can move those funds until admin takes action
  • Admin can: Release (payment confirmed → crypto goes to buyer) or Refund (payment rejected / fraud → crypto returns to seller)
  • In a dispute, funds remain frozen until the dispute is resolved
💡 PakSwap uses an internal ledger for escrow — funds never leave the platform during a trade. The blockchain transaction only occurs when you withdraw to an external wallet.

Common Problems

My timer expired but I already paid

Open a dispute immediately from the trade page. Provide your payment screenshot. Admin will investigate and release the crypto if payment is confirmed.

The seller is unresponsive

Use the in-trade chat. If there is no response within 30 minutes of uploading your payment screenshot, click Open Dispute. Admin will step in.

I accidentally sent the wrong amount

Contact the seller via trade chat immediately. If the seller agrees, they may manually confirm. Otherwise, open a dispute so admin can review both amounts and decide the correct resolution.

I see "Payment Under Review" for a long time

During peak hours (6pm–10pm PKT), review times may extend to 30–60 minutes. If it's been over 60 minutes, contact support via the Help button in the trade window.
